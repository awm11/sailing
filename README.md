# Close Hauled

A top-down sailing game in a single HTML file. Steer with the rudder, trim the sail, balance the boat and read the wind from the water.

**Play:** https://awm11.github.io/sailing/

## Modes
- **Open water**: free sailing in wind that shifts and changes strength, with seven drills that teach the points of sail.
- **Race course**: race three computer-sailed boats. Time your start to a 40-second countdown (cross early and you must go back), round three marks keeping them on your left, obey the right-of-way rules, and finish first.

## Wind strength
Choose **Light**, **Moderate** or **Strong** on the menu. It sets the wind range, how much the wind shifts, how strong the gusts are and how easily you capsize. Best times are kept for each level.

## Sailing features
- Realistic sail forces from the apparent wind: luffing, stalling, the no-go zone, tacking and gybing.
- Heel and balance: lean your weight out to keep the boat flat. Lean too little in a gust and you capsize; swim to the centreboard and lean back to right the boat.
- Wind you can read from the water: wave crests, streaks and darker gust patches. A compass dial shows the wind zones around your boat.
- Seaweed beds that slow you down, rocks, an island, moored boats and fixed floats as landmarks, all drawn in an angled 3D view.
- Right of way: port tack gives way to starboard; on the same tack the windward boat keeps clear. Fouls cost 10 seconds.

## Controls
| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ← → (A D) | Rudder: steer left / right |
| ↑ ↓ (W S) | Pull the sail in / let it out |
| Q E, or two-finger sideways swipe | Lean your weight left / right to balance the boat |
| I | Reverse the swipe direction |
| Space (hold) | Right the boat after a capsize |
| T | Auto-trim the sail |
| H / P / R / M | Tips / pause / restart / menu |
| ← → on the menu | Change wind strength |

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a browser. There is no build step.
